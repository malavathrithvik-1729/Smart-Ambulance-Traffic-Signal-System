📖 How to Use & Demo Guide

Team Lifeline — Smart Ambulance Traffic Signal System • SPR High School, Kamareddy

⚠ Demo

This is a prototype built for the school science exhibition. All ambulance movement, signal clearance & calls are simulated in the browser. A real IoT-connected version is coming soon!

🔬 How Our Project Works
Layer 1 — Caller App
Report Emergency Person at accident scene speaks or types the emergency and shares GPS location.
Layer 2 — Driver App
Navigate to Scene Ambulance driver sees optimised route and a smart green signal corridor activates ahead.
Layer 3 — Smart Signals
Green Corridor Signals within 280 m turn GREEN and revert to RED after 350 m. Zero stops for the ambulance.
🧪 Step-by-Step: How to Test the Demo

Need: Google Chrome + internet. Open two Chrome windows side by side for best experience.

PHASE 1 — Setup
1
Open two Chrome windows side by side
Press Ctrl+N to open a second Chrome window. Window 1 = Caller/Victim. Window 2 = Ambulance Driver.
2
Open ambulance.html in Window 2
In the second window, open ambulance.html — keep it visible alongside this page.
PHASE 2 — Report the Emergency (Window 1 — This Page)
3
Describe the emergency
Click 🎙️ Hold to Speak and say the emergency, e.g. "Accident near Kamareddy bus stand, one person injured." — OR type it in the textarea below.
💡 Voice needs microphone permission in Chrome. If unavailable, just type.
4
Share your location
Click 📍 Use My Location and Allow. Coordinates turn green. If denied, Kamareddy demo coordinates are used automatically — that's fine.
5
Press "🚨 CALL FOR EMERGENCY"
Button turns bright green once description + location are ready. Click it — page navigates to the Victim Dashboard (victim.html) automatically.
PHASE 3 — Driver Dashboard (Window 2)
6
Check incident & review routes on ambulance.html
Your emergency description appears in the red sidebar card. Two routes show on the map — green solid (Route 1, BEST) and amber dashed (Route 2). Click either card to switch.
7
Press "🚀 Go — Start Journey"
Ambulance marker starts moving on the map. Switch to Window 1 — victim dashboard should show "Ambulance En Route" with live ETA countdown.
PHASE 4 — Smart Signals & AI Chatbot
8
Watch signal icons turn green on the driver map
As ambulance approaches within 280 m of a signal it turns 🟢 GREEN. After passing 350 m it reverts to 🔴 RED. Voice announces each junction.
9
Test the First-Aid AI chatbot (victim.html)
Click 🩸 Bleeding, 🫀 Unconscious, 🦴 Fracture, or 🚗 Crash for instant first-aid steps. Or type any question and press Ask.
10
Test the in-app call feature
Press "📞 Talk with Ambulance Team" on victim.html or "📞 Call Victim" on ambulance.html. A simulated conversation shows and is read aloud by text-to-speech.
💡 Unmute speakers to hear the voice announcement.
PHASE 5 — Arrival & Hospital Transfer
11
Ambulance arrives at scene (~25 sec)
Victim.html shows "Ambulance Has Arrived!" overlay. Driver.html shows the Select Hospital card with ICU beds and OT status.
12
Select hospital & transfer patient
Click "🏥 Select & View Routes" on the recommended hospital → choose Route 1 Direct → press "🏥 Go — Transfer Patient". Smart signals activate again for the hospital route.
✓
Mission Complete! 🎉
Driver dashboard shows "Patient Delivered!". Click "← New Emergency" to restart the demo from the beginning.
