Team Lifeline — Smart Ambulance Traffic Signal System • SPR High School, Kamareddy

⚠ Demo

This is a prototype built for the school science exhibition. All ambulance movement, signal clearance & calls are simulated in the browser. A real IoT-connected version is coming soon!

🔬 How Our Project Works
Layer 1 — Caller App
Report Emergency Person at accident scene speaks or types the emergency and shares GPS location.
Layer 2 — Driver App
Navigate to Scene Ambulance driver sees optimised route and a smart green signal corridor activates ahead.
Layer 3 — Smart Signals
Green Corridor Signals within 280 m turn GREEN and revert to RED after 350 m. Zero stops for the ambulance.
🧪 Step-by-Step: How to Test the Demo

Need: Google Chrome + internet. Open two Chrome windows side by side for best experience.

PHASE 1 — Setup
1
Open two Chrome windows side by side
Press Ctrl+N for a second window. Window 1 = Caller/Victim • Window 2 = Ambulance Driver.
2
Open ambulance.html in Window 2
Keep it visible alongside this page — they communicate in real time.
PHASE 2 — Report the Emergency (Window 1)
3
Describe the emergency
Click 🎙️ Hold to Speak and say the emergency — or type it in the textarea below.
💡 Voice needs microphone permission. If unavailable, just type.
4
Share your location
Click 📍 Use My Location → Allow. If denied, Kamareddy demo coordinates are used automatically.
5
Press "🚨 CALL FOR EMERGENCY"
Button turns green when both fields are ready. Click it — page navigates to the Victim Dashboard automatically.
PHASE 3 — Driver Dashboard (Window 2)
6
Check incident & review routes on ambulance.html
Your emergency description appears in the red sidebar card. Two routes on the map — green (Route 1 BEST) and amber (Route 2). Click cards to switch.
7
Press "🚀 Go — Start Journey"
Ambulance starts moving. Switch to Window 1 — victim dashboard shows "Ambulance En Route" with live ETA.
PHASE 4 — Smart Signals & AI Chatbot
8
Watch signal icons turn green on the driver map
Within 280 m → 🟢 GREEN. After 350 m past → 🔴 RED. Voice announces each junction.
9
Test First-Aid AI chatbot & in-app call
On victim.html click 🩸 Bleeding, 🫀 Unconscious, 🦴 Fracture, 🚗 Crash for instant first-aid. Also test the 📞 call button on either page.
PHASE 5 — Arrival & Hospital Transfer
10
Ambulance arrives → select hospital → transfer patient
Victim.html shows "Ambulance Has Arrived!". Driver selects hospital → route → presses "🏥 Go — Transfer Patient".
✓
Mission Complete! 🎉
Driver shows "Patient Delivered!". Click "← New Emergency" to restart the demo.

🔬 Project Details

Team Lifeline — Smart Ambulance Traffic Signal System • SPR High School, Kamareddy

⚠️ Problem Statement

Every minute counts in a medical emergency — and traffic signals are killing people.

  • In India, over 1.5 lakh people die annually in road accidents. Many die waiting for ambulances stuck in traffic.
  • Ambulances in small cities like Kamareddy have no dedicated signal clearance system — they depend on honking and luck.
  • There is no real-time link between the accident victim, the ambulance driver, and the traffic infrastructure.
  • Victims have no first-aid guidance during the critical minutes before the ambulance arrives.
  • Emergency call systems in India are slow — manual dispatch adds 3–8 minutes of avoidable delay.
✅ Our Solution

Team Lifeline — A unified digital emergency response system that removes all friction between accident and hospital.

🎙️ Instant Emergency Reporting
Voice-to-text emergency description + GPS location captured in under 10 seconds. One tap dispatches the ambulance.
🚦 Smart Signal Clearance
Proximity algorithm turns signals green within 280 m of the ambulance, restoring normal traffic after it passes.
🤖 First-Aid AI Chatbot
Provides step-by-step first-aid guidance for bleeding, fractures, CPR, and crashes during the wait time.
🏥 Hospital-to-Driver Link
Driver selects hospital with live ICU and OT availability, routes navigate with smart signal clearance all the way.

🛠️ Technology Used
Frontend
HTML5 • CSS3 • Vanilla JavaScript
Web Speech API (voice input)
Geolocation API (GPS capture)
BroadcastChannel API (real-time sync)
Mapping & Routing
Leaflet.js (interactive maps)
OpenStreetMap (map tiles)
OSRM API (real road routing)
Haversine formula (distance calc)
Smart Signal Algorithm
Proximity trigger at 280 m (green)
Reset trigger at 350 m (red)
Moving corridor — only 1–2 green at once
Voice announcement via Web Speech API
Future Stack (v2.0)
Raspberry Pi / Arduino (IoT signals)
4G/5G GPS module in ambulance
Firebase / Node.js backend
Android & iOS mobile apps
